Project Supervision

I am supervising a number of students on their final year project for the BSc/MSci Mathematics degree and MSc Statistics students on their dissertation at Imperial College London. If you are a final-year or MSc student interested in my work and would like to collaborate on a project, feel free to contact me via e-mail.
I have been an Associate Fellow of Advance HE (AFHEA) since February 2024. I am also an ambassador of the MSc Statistics programme at Imperial College London, in recognition of outstanding contributions to the running and delivery of the programme.

In the academic year 2024-2025, I was a highly commended nominee for the Faculty of Natural Sciences (FoNS) Prize for Excellence in teaching and learning at the FoNS Annual Prizes 2025.

During my PhD at Imperial College London, I have been actively involved in several undergraduate & postgraduate courses as a (Senior) Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) or as a marker (list of modules below).
